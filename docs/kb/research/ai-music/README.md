# AI music research (checked 2026-10-31)

Research behind the [AI Music Session Guide](../../apps/ai-music-guide.md). Four logs written by research subagents, each with sources, conflicts, myths and gaps.

- [suno.md](suno.md): Suno as of October 2026. Covers the v6 family (all older models retired on 9 Sep 2026), plans, credits and download caps, every create and edit feature, Studio 2.0, the tag and punctuation lore with reliability notes, style-box wording, failure modes and fixes, iteration counts, stems, rights and label deals.
- [platforms.md](platforms.md): every other generator, including Udio (no downloads since October 2025), ElevenLabs Music v2.5, Lyria 3.5 and Flow Music, Mureka, Stable Audio 3, MiniMax 3.0 and the open models. Also how prompting differs between them, the Artificial Analysis music arena, and lawsuits and licensing.
- [stems-cleanup.md](stems-cleanup.md): MVSep separation scores, UVR and Demucs commands, commercial and DAW separators, the order of operations, a symptom → cause → fix table for AI artifacts, MIDI extraction, and loudness targets and mastering.
- [technique.md](technique.md): what charting and viral AI acts did, with acts anonymised as Act A to P. Also the anatomy of a modern hit, platform, distributor and chart rules, US copyright, and a basics-to-pro ladder.

**Naming rule:** the guide names no AI artists, so it does not steer readers toward or away from any act. The technique log keys acts by letter. Sources whose URL contains an act's name stay in the logs and are not linked from the app.

**Biggest gaps:**
- No platform publishes keeper rates.
- Tag reliability on v6 rests on anecdotes; the tested data comes from v3.5 to v5.5.
- Nobody has published the cause of "Suno shimmer".
- There are no first-person workflow interviews with the charting acts.
