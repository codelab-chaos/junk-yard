How to read this guide

Technique, then result

Every line is something to do, with what you should hear when it works in the You hear column. Open a line for steps, an example to copy, what to watch for, and related entries.

Levels

Basics work inside the generator in one sitting. Intermediate steers it with tags, extends and section edits. Advanced takes stems into an editor. Pro rebuilds, re-records, masters and releases.

Reliable, hit and miss, myth

Tags and tips carry a verdict. Reliable works on most generations with current models. Hit and miss works sometimes; regenerate or reinforce it. Myth is common advice that does not hold up.

Terms

Stem: one part of a song on its own track (vocal, drums, bass). DAW: an audio editor such as Logic, Ableton or Reaper. LUFS: average loudness; streaming plays at about -14. dBTP: true peak level. SDR: a separation score in dB; higher is cleaner.

Point in time

Checked on 31 October 2026. Generators change models and prices every few months, and old tricks stop working. No artists are named: the guide studies what popular AI releases do, not who made them.
